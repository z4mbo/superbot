People, bots and permissions

A plugin never acts on its own. A reviewer approves each version, a person installs it for themselves, grants each part to each bot, and approves every write a bot proposes.

Who can do what

RoleCanHow they get it
PublisherSubmit versions under a slug they reserved, see their status and rejection reasonsAny signed-in account. The first submission reserves the slug
ReviewerSee the review queue, approve or reject a pending version onceA verified account listed in SUPERBOT_ADMIN_EMAILS by the server operator
InstallerInstall an approved version in the selected Space, grant parts to bots, switch versions, uninstall, use record appsAny member of the Space. The installation belongs to their account
BotUse only the parts granted to it, within the limits belowA grant from the installer

Installations are per account and per Space. Another member of the same Space doesn't see your installation, your grants or your record app entries. Leaving the Space removes your installations there.

Grants

  • A new installation has no grants. A grant gives one bot one part.
  • Setting grants replaces that bot's whole list for the installation. An empty list revokes them all.
  • Removing a grant and adding it back creates a new grant. Approvals still waiting on the old one can't authorize the new one.
  • Switching to another version clears every grant, so you review the new version before granting again.
  • The server checks the live grant again after each approval and before each call. A request already sent to a tool server may still finish after you revoke.

Approvals

Each Space has an approval mode: Ask, Auto or Never ask. Plugin actions follow it with stricter rules:

Bot actionAsk and AutoNever ask
Read a skillRunsRuns
List record app entriesRunsRuns
Add a record app entryAsks every timeRefused
Call a tool on an MCP serverAsks every timeRefused

Writes and tool calls offer only Allow once: there is no rule that allows them in advance, and automatic review never answers for them. The card shows the destination or app and the exact values. Arguments too long to review are refused before the card appears.

Record app access

  • People who installed the app can list, add and remove its entries. Bots can list and propose additions, never remove.
  • A bot gets record app actions only in a direct chat with the installer, in a turn the installer's own message started. Group conversations and turns started by anyone else get none.
  • The bot must belong to the installer, in the same Space.
  • Turning off a grant keeps the entries. Uninstalling deletes them.

Local models

When a bot answers through a model CLI installed on your computer, Superbot gives that CLI no tools. You decide what it sees instead:

  • Terminal: in superbot BOT_NAME, type /apps, then /app 1 --date 2026-11-20 How many calories did I log?. Review a proposed entry and type save, or press Enter to skip. /app retry repeats a save that wasn't confirmed.

The model receives your request, the app's fields, the five most recent matching entries (at most 24 KB) and the total. The exchange stays out of the shared conversation. This works only with local models that support it; others get App assistance requires a selected prompt-only local model.

Ask for less

  • Split a plugin into parts by what a bot needs, so people can grant only that. The T3 Code inbox keeps receipts in a part no bot is granted.
  • Declare only the fields you need. Bots see every field of a granted app.
  • Keep tool arguments short and specific. People read them before every call.
  • Put no secrets in skills or field labels. Skills are untrusted input to the bot, and a manifest can't carry credentials.

Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat it meansWhat to do
The bot says the plugin tool is unavailableThe grant was removed, the version changed, or the call came from a group chat or someone else's turnCheck the grant under Installed and ask in your direct chat
Writes are refused without a cardThe Space is set to Never askSwitch the Space to Ask or Auto
A grant disappeared after an updateSwitching versions clears grantsReview the new version and grant again
FORBIDDEN on reviewYour account isn't a verified deployment adminAsk the operator to add it to SUPERBOT_ADMIN_EMAILS
