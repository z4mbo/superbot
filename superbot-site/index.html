Apps you and your bots keep together

A record app is a list of fields in a plugin manifest. Superbot stores the entries and draws the form and totals. Your bot reads the same entries and asks before adding one.

  • Opt in, per personInstall an app for your own account in a Space. Other members never see your entries.
  • One bot at a timeGrant the app to the bots you choose. They can list entries and propose new ones in your direct chats.
  • Every addition approvedA bot's entry is saved only after you check its values and choose Allow once. Only people remove entries.

A plugin is one small manifest

No packages, no install scripts, no secrets. A plugin describes what it adds; the server checks it and a reviewer approves it.

  • SkillsMarkdown instructions that teach a bot how to do something. Bots treat them as untrusted input.
  • ToolsA remote MCP server on a public HTTPS address. Before every call, a person sees the destination and the arguments and chooses Allow once.
  • Record appsA list of fields, such as a date, a meal and its calories. Superbot stores the entries and shows its own form, list and total. No code, no server.
  • Grants per botInstalling adds nothing to any bot. People switch on each part for each bot they choose.
  • Pinned versionsApproved versions never change. An installation stays on its version until someone switches it.
